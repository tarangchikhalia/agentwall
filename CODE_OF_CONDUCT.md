# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or other protected characteristic.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Learning from experience
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community
- Communicating technical disagreements professionally and constructively

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission
- Threats or encouragement of violence
- Intimidation or stalking
- Deliberate disruption of community discussions
- Repeated unwanted communication
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

Technical disagreement is not itself a violation of this Code of Conduct. Contributors are encouraged to challenge ideas, designs, and implementations while remaining respectful toward the people involved.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing the project.

Examples of project spaces include:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Code reviews
- Documentation
- Chat systems
- Mailing lists
- Community forums
- Conferences or project events

Representing the project includes using an official project account, acting as an appointed project representative, or participating in an event in an official project capacity.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, documentation, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation by contributors whose behavior is considered inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Enforcement decisions should be made consistently and proportionately.

The privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected to the extent reasonably possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may use the following guidelines when determining consequences for behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior considered unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private or public warning explaining the violation and requesting corrective behavior.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A formal warning with conditions for continued participation. Interaction with involved individuals may be restricted for a specified period.

### 3. Temporary Restriction

**Community Impact:** A serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** Temporary restriction from project interaction, which may include commenting, reviewing, opening issues or pull requests, participating in discussions, or attending project events.

### 4. Permanent Restriction

**Community Impact:** A pattern of violations, harassment, threats, aggression, or other conduct demonstrating that continued participation presents significant risk to the community.

**Consequence:** Permanent removal from participation in project spaces where technically and organizationally possible.

## Conflicts of Interest

A maintainer who is directly involved in a reported incident should not be solely responsible for deciding its outcome.

When possible, another maintainer or appropriate neutral party should review the report.

## Good-Faith Reports

Reports made in good faith will not result in retaliation.

Knowingly false reports, deliberate misrepresentation, or use of the reporting process to harass others may themselves violate this Code of Conduct.
